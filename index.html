Scheduling assumptions

Monday–Friday calendar, no holidays. Durations are working-day boundaries: a five-day task beginning Monday finishes at the next Monday boundary. Task start is the maximum of predecessor gate completion and required component availability. A supplier delay shifts the component availability for every mapped task. Other changes add time to one selected task. Approval is a sequential gate after that task; its successors wait.

Cost & approval assumptions

Direct scenario cost includes entered change and approval costs; acceleration adds its premium. Approval durations use the longest required customer lead time and summed required costs. These are estimates, not regulatory determinations. Review/unknown policies remain unresolved. Component replacements never receive automatic engineering approval.

EOL bridge calculation

Coverage = usable units / monthly demand. Bridge purchase cost = last-time-buy units × price. Inventory cash is shown separately from redesign costs. Stock is not guaranteed. No stock expiry, yield, multiple units per camera, carrying cost, future demand changes, or other product consumption is modeled. Zero demand produces unknown coverage.

Limits & next production requirements

One scenario at a time; baseline work has not started. No resource leveling, progress tracking, multi-project allocation, or probabilistic forecast. CSV, JSON and pasted text are supported; PDF and email connectors are not implemented. Live model calls require a server-side API connection. Team synchronization and cross-device persistence require a database. Browser data can be lost; export backups.

Evidence & privacy

Local parsing does not send data to an AI provider. Downloading a prompt does not send it; you choose where to use it. Model output must quote the provided source and cannot directly change schedules without confirmation. Source quotations demonstrate provenance, not factual correctness. Sample companies and data are synthetic; no customer research or measured employer impact is claimed.
